A pixel-based redesign of Rami Portis's album Plonter.

A new design interpretation for one of Rami Portis's best-known albums, with a redesigned record, cassette and lyric booklet, alongside a full visual and conceptual direction.

The concept

Pixels for a world glued to screens.

I chose to redesign "Plonter" ("Tangle") to connect the spirit of the album with today's digital reality. The visual language is built from pixels, and every song gets its own visual interpretation.

The concept grows out of the song "Get Off My TV Screen" and its take on our addiction to media and screens, which feels just as relevant today.
