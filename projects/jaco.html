An educational board game for elementary school kids.

Jakko is an English-learning board game for children in elementary school, built so that new words come through play rather than drills.

01
The game

Path, coins and new words.

The game combines progress along a path and collecting coins with language practice, so kids learn vocabulary in an intuitive, pressure-free way.

Double-sided cards and visual storytelling turn learning a language into something fun, sparking curiosity, confidence and natural progress.

Why Jakko?

The name is inspired by the African grey parrot, called ג׳אקו in Hebrew. It’s considered one of the most intelligent birds in the world, known for imitating human speech, recognising words and learning through communication and imitation.

Next to it are the monkey and the dolphin, two more animals known for their intelligence, curiosity and ability to learn. Together, the three carry the values of the game and the idea behind it: learning English through play, interaction and communication.

The parrot gave the game its name because of its direct, unique connection to language and speech, and became the leading symbol of the learning experience.

02
The method

Meet Hebrish: say it before you read it.

For Jakko I developed a method I call Hebrish: English words written in Hebrew letters with nikud, spelled the way the English word sounds. A child who can’t read “Apple” yet can already read אֶפֶּל, say it out loud and learn the word, long before reading it in English.

Hebrish אֶפֶּל

Same sound, letters kids already know.

03
Game components

Question on the front, answer on the back.

Every card has two sides. Hover or tap a card to flip it, just like checking the answer at the table.

04
How to play

So, how do you win in English?

Jakko is here with you! Along the way you travel the winding path around the island and get to know new letters and words in English.

20coins
The goal

Answer as many cards correctly as you can to collect coins.

The first to reach 20 coins wins!

Getting started
  1. 01

    Every player gets a pawn to move along the path, plus 1 coin for the road.

  2. 02

    On your turn, roll the die and move forward by the number you rolled.

  3. 03

    Land on a “Draw a card” square? Take a card from the pile that matches the square’s colour.

  4. 04

    Got it right? Well done! The card’s colour tells you how many coins you’ve won. Keep them on the coin board under your pawn.

  5. 05

    Not sure, or got it wrong? Check the answer on the back, then slip the card back into its pile, question side up.

Special squares

Lifebuoy

Landing here earns you a lifebuoy. Save it for one of two moments:

  • When you’re stuck on a card you can’t answer, you still win its coins.
  • When another player tries a “coin heist” on you, you’re protected.

Go diving

Head to the diving zone on the board and wait there for one round.

Hop X steps

Jump back or forward, right away, in the same turn.

Surprise card

Draw from the surprise pile, do what it says, then tuck it back under the pile.

Start

Every time you pass the start square, you earn 1 coin.
