About me

Hi, I'm Aviv.
Nice to meet you.

Product & Visual Designer

I'm a designer and a graduate of Visual Communication at Shenkar Handesaim, with honors, and I love creating things that move people. As a kid, I'd stay up at night to watch Apple's yearly keynote, waiting for the new iPhone and getting excited by every video that showed what was about to arrive. Without knowing it then, I was drawn to the way design can build anticipation, spark curiosity and turn a product into an experience. Over the years I realized I want to be on the side that creates that moment.

Somewhere between being a dance major in high school and my first designs in Canva, I found out that creating and designing is much more than a hobby. Since then, I love taking a small idea and turning it into a whole world, with a strong visual language, a clear character and a look that's hard to ignore.

For me, design is much more than aesthetics. It's a way to create a feeling, to spark curiosity and to connect people to an idea. I'm drawn to projects with thought, personality and a story behind them, that also know how to be precise, useful and relevant in the real world. Whether it's a brand or an interface, I'm always looking for the point where precision, emotion and creativity meet. That's where you'll find me too.
